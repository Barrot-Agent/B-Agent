# The Real Tech Stack — What You Need and What You Don't

**Premise.** Most stacks are too big. The minimum viable stack is five components.

## Beats

1. The five: model access, execution surface, job store, artifact store, observability.
2. What you do not need on day one: a vector database, a queue cluster, a Kubernetes install.
3. Build vs buy: buy the model and the computer, build the skills and the fence.
4. Where the repo lives and why it matters (versioned procedures, reviewable diffs).
5. Secrets management for a one-person shop: env injection, no secrets in prompts or files.
6. The upgrade path when volume 10x's: what to split first.

## What to measure

- Every component you add is a failure mode you now own at 3 a.m.

## Do not

- Do not recommend a stack you have not run. Name the tradeoff of each choice.
