# Security, Privacy & Risk — Don't Let Your Grok Bot Become a $50K Mistake

**Premise.** The attack surface is the permission set. Shrink it until the blast radius is boring.

## Beats

1. Threat model for a one-person shop: credential theft, prompt injection, data exfiltration, destructive tool call.
2. Prompt injection is a data problem: untrusted text never gets authority; tools are allowlisted server-side.
3. Least privilege in practice: per-agent tokens, read-only by default, separate credentials per customer.
4. Data handling: what leaves the building, retention, deletion, and the one-page policy you can send a client.
5. Blast radius: sandbox the filesystem, cap spend, two-phase deletes, no production writes without approval.
6. Incident response: detect, contain, notify, fix, write the regression test.

## What to measure

- Recovery time matters more than prevention claims; test revocation quarterly.

## Do not

- Do not claim compliance you have not audited. Say what you do and what you don't.
