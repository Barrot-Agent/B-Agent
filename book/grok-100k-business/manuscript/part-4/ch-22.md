# Delivery — Let Agents Do the Work While You Watch Exceptions

**Premise.** Delivery is a queue with a service level, not a heroic effort.

## Beats

1. The intake contract: what the customer sends, in what format, by when.
2. The pipeline: ingest → execute → verify → package → deliver, each with a status.
3. Service levels and what happens when they slip.
4. The weekly digest: what was done, what was flagged, what is pending.
5. Onboarding in one day: credentials, sample data, first deliverable.
6. Offboarding in one hour: revoke, export, delete, confirm in writing.

## What to measure

- Measure cycle time per deliverable and exception rate per customer; both trend down or the account is at risk.

## Do not

- Do not accept unstructured inputs at scale without a normalization step.
