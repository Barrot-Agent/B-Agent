# The $100K/Month Run Rate — The Numbers That Make This Model Work

**Premise.** Run rate is a trajectory, not a month. Prove the trajectory with cohort data.

## Beats

1. MRR, net new, churn, expansion: the four lines that fit on one screen.
2. Cohort retention: does month 6 look like month 1?
3. Capacity model: exception hours vs available hours, per customer.
4. The hiring trigger: the exact point where a human specialist beats another agent.
5. Scenario table: 50/100/200 customers at constant quality.
6. The honest ceiling: what breaks first at 3x volume, and the fix.

## What to measure

- At 5% monthly churn the base halves every 14 months; retention is the growth lever.

## Do not

- Do not present a single-point forecast. Show three scenarios.
