# Find the Market — Where Businesses Already Bleed Time and Money

**Premise.** Find a job with a budget line, not a pain with an audience.

## Beats

1. Three market filters: existing spend, measurable delay, tolerance for a new vendor.
2. Where agent-shaped work already has a budget: agency retainers, VA hours, bookkeeping fees, research subscriptions.
3. Sizing a niche by counting buyers, not by citing a TAM report.
4. The 20-conversation discovery sprint: what to ask, what not to pitch.
5. Signals of a bleeding process: job postings, spreadsheet handoffs, 'we do it manually'.
6. Choosing between vertical depth and horizontal function.

## What to measure

- A niche is real if you can name 50 buyers and two of them have already paid someone for this.

## Do not

- Do not fabricate market size statistics. Count what you can count.
