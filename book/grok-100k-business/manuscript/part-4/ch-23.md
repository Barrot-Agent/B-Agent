# Customer Support Without a Support Team — Let the Agents Take the Queue

**Premise.** Support is triage plus trust. Automate the first, never fake the second.

## Beats

1. The queue taxonomy: how-to, broken, billing, angry, legal. Route before you answer.
2. Retrieval-augmented answers with a citation to your own docs; no citation, no send.
3. Escalation rules that fire on sentiment, repetition and money words.
4. The honesty line: disclose automation, never impersonate a person.
5. Response time as a product feature; measure first-response and resolution.
6. The knowledge loop: every escalated ticket becomes a doc entry or a skill fix.

## What to measure

- Deflection rate without a quality check is a vanity metric; pair it with reopen rate.

## Do not

- Do not let an agent promise refunds, timelines or policy exceptions.
