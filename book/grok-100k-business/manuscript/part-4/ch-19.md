# Stop Selling Grok Bot — Sell the Finished Job

**Premise.** Buyers do not want an agent. They want the thing that was on someone's to-do list.

## Beats

1. The offer sentence: outcome, timeframe, evidence, price. No technology nouns.
2. Why 'AI agent' loses to 'your books closed by Friday' in every sales conversation.
3. Proof assets: a sample deliverable, an exception log, a reference call.
4. Risk reversal: a defined revision policy, a kill switch, data handling in one paragraph.
5. The three objections and the honest answers: accuracy, data, 'what if you disappear'.
6. Pricing the outcome, not the seat (bridge to Chapter 21).

## What to measure

- One customer, one deliverable, one number in the contract.

## Do not

- Do not promise autonomy. Promise a result with a named reviewer.
