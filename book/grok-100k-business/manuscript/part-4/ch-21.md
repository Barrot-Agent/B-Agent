# Packaging & Pricing — Charge for Results, Not Tokens

**Premise.** Price against the replacement cost, not against your inference bill.

## Beats

1. The floor: 3–5x fully loaded cost, where cost includes review time.
2. Three packages that map to buyer maturity: done-with-you, done-for-you, done-and-monitored.
3. Unit of pricing: per deliverable, per seat served, per outcome; never per token.
4. Annual prepay as a cash-flow tool, with a cancellation clause you can survive.
5. Discount discipline: discount scope, never accuracy.
6. The price increase script for existing customers.

## What to measure

- Replacement cost anchor: hours/month × loaded hourly rate, then take 40–60%.

## Do not

- Do not price below the cost of your own review time. That is how operators burn out at 40 customers.
