# Week 4: Price It, Prove It, Repeat It

**Premise.** Proposed chapter — first invoice, first reference, first repeatable offer.

## Beats

1. Compute fully loaded cost per deliverable.
2. Set price at 3–5x; write the contract in one page.
3. Deliver for one customer for one month.
4. Collect the reference and the exception log; decide on employee #2.
