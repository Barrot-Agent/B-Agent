# Grok Bot — Put a Brain Behind the Business

**Premise.** The model is the planning layer: it decides what to do next, not what to say next.

## Beats

1. What a Bot is documented to be: a proactive teammate with a real cloud computer (browser, filesystem, terminal) that works across apps and sites.
2. Brain vs chat: the model's job in an agent loop — decompose, choose a tool, read the result, decide again.
3. Choosing a model: Grok 4.7 at 500K context, $2/M in and $6/M out under 200K prompt tokens; grok-build-0.1 at 256K context for code-shaped work at $1/$2.
4. Long-context pricing is a cliff, not a slope: at 200K+ prompt tokens every token doubles.
5. What the brain should never decide: whether it may act (Chapter 12), and whether its own output is correct (Chapter 28).
6. Instrumentation: log the prompt size, the tool calls and the stop reason for every run, or the cost model is fiction.

## What to measure

- Cost per run at 12K in / 2K out on grok-4.7: $0.036 before tools.
- Same run with 40 web searches: +$0.20, i.e. tools dominate by 5x.

## Do not

- Do not claim benchmark scores. Cite only documented limits and prices.
