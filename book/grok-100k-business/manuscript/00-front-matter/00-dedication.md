# Dedication

For the operator who is still the bottleneck.

You already know the work. You already know which hour of the day is wasted. What
you do not have is a second pair of hands that does not need a salary, a
one-on-one, or a two-week notice.

This book is about building that pair of hands properly — with a fence around it,
a ledger behind it, and a price in front of it.
