# Your First Employee — Pick One Expensive Job to Automate

**Premise.** Not the easiest job. The most expensive one that passes the readiness score.

## Beats

1. The job inventory: list every recurring task with hours/month, error cost and reversibility.
2. Rank by `hours × hourly cost × repeatability`, then filter by readiness score (Chapter 6).
3. The first employee should produce an artifact you can diff, not a conversation.
4. Scope discipline: one job, one input format, one output format, one customer.
5. The two-week contract: what you will measure, what counts as success, when you stop.
6. What to do when the first job turns out to be three jobs (it will).

## What to measure

- 50 real items, scored by hand, before any customer sees output.

## Do not

- Do not start with the job you enjoy. Start with the one that costs the most.
