# Content Agent — Write, Design, Publish, Repurpose, Repeat

**Premise.** One source asset, many formats, one human approval before anything ships.

## Beats

1. The source-of-truth asset: one long piece per week, everything else derived.
2. Format ladder: article → thread → carousel → newsletter → short video script.
3. Voice control: a style file with five good samples and five bad ones beats adjectives like 'punchy'.
4. Publishing gates: factual claims checked, links live, brand assets current, approval logged.
5. Repurposing without drift: the derivative must not invent claims absent from the source.
6. The calendar as a fence: scheduled, idempotent, cancelable.

## What to measure

- Measure edit distance: words changed per 1,000 drafted. Falling edit distance is the quality signal.

## Do not

- Do not publish without a named human approval on record.

## Draft checklist

- [ ] Every beat above covered by a section
- [ ] Claims labeled documented / measured / assumed
- [ ] At least one worked number
- [ ] No invented product capabilities
