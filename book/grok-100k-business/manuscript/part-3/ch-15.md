# Research Agent — Turn the Web Into a Research Department

**Premise.** The deliverable is a memo with citations and a confidence line, not a summary.

## Beats

1. Question decomposition: a research request becomes 5–12 answerable sub-questions.
2. Source discipline: primary over secondary, date-stamped, quoted with links.
3. The memo schema: answer, evidence table, contradictions, confidence, what would change the answer.
4. Cost control: search is the expensive tool; cap calls per memo and measure yield per call.
5. Staleness: every fact carries a date; anything older than the domain's half-life is flagged.
6. Quality gate: a second pass that tries to falsify the top three claims.

## What to measure

- 20 searches at $5/1k = $0.10; that usually exceeds the token cost of the memo.

## Do not

- Do not allow uncited assertions in a memo that will be sold. One hallucinated citation kills the account.
