# Sales Agent — Find Leads, Research Them, Write Outreach, Book Calls

**Premise.** The sales agent does not sell. It removes everything between you and the conversation.

## Beats

1. Pipeline stages and which ones are automatable: sourcing yes, research yes, drafting yes, sending gated, closing no.
2. Lead sourcing with MCP: search, enrichment, dedupe; the cost per qualified lead.
3. Research brief format: one page, five facts, one hook, one reason to say no.
4. Outreach drafting rules: specificity, one ask, no claims you cannot evidence, unsubscribe path.
5. Booking: calendar tool + confirmation + no-show follow-up; the agent never negotiates price.
6. Metrics: reply rate, positive rate, meeting rate, cost per meeting; the funnel arithmetic from Chapter 4.

## What to measure

- Cold outbound assumption set: 1,000 contacts → 4% reply → 20% calls → 25% close = 2 customers.

## Do not

- Do not write copy about capabilities the product does not have. Verify before send.
