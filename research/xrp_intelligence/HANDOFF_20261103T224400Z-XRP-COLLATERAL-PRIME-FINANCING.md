## Coinbase Global Exchange
Coinbase's current help pages explicitly list XRP among accepted collateral assets for perpetual futures, in addition to USDC. Limits are applied at organization/UBO level using average daily quantity (ADQ); collateral value is notional multiplied by a weight after the greater of base and closeout-horizon haircuts. Excess above ADQ limits does not count toward portfolio value. Collateral can be liquidated automatically below maintenance margin.

The XRP-PERP product is USDC-settled. Product specifications show up to 50x maximum leverage and 25% default initial margin (4x). These are product limits, not typical or recommended leverage. Coinbase does not disclose XRP collateral balances or utilization in the reviewed help pages.

## Ripple Prime
Its current official product page lists RLUSD, XRP, tokenized real-world assets and traditional assets as postable collateral in a cross-margin pool. Ripple Prime also launched U.S. OTC spot prime brokerage in XRP and RLUSD and can cross-margin eligible holdings/derivatives. This establishes product-level support, not actual XRP collateral balances or XRP-backed loan volumes.

## Financing
Ripple Prime closed a Neuberger Specialty Finance facility of up to $200M; Ripple says proceeds extend financing to clients and increase lending capacity. Actual drawdown is not disclosed. A separate $275M senior unsecured notes placement closed in August 2026 for working capital/general purposes. The $475M nominal sum is a facility plus issued notes, not $475M of XRP purchases or confirmed deployed credit.

## Demand interpretation
Collateral eligibility can make XRP more useful to institutions and reduce the need to sell existing holdings for margin. It does not prove new spot purchases. Actual XRP collateral balances, haircuts, concentration utilization, loan notional, liquidations and time-series changes remain unknown.

Do not confuse RLUSD collateral use in Ripple Prime/repo markets with XRP collateral. Do not confuse XRP eligibility with actual posted XRP. No directional forecast is issued from these announcements.
