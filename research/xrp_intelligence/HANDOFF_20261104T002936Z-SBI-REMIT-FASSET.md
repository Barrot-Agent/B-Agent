# SBI Remit: XRP and Fasset stablecoin rails — 2026-11-04

## Confirmed scale

SBI Remit's official group profile reports 82.96% SBI Group voting-rights ownership as of August 28, 2026 and coverage of approximately 200 countries/regions. SBI Remit reported cumulative remittance handling above **¥2.5 trillion as of May 29, 2026**.

That ¥2.5T is total remittance handling across channels and corridors. It is **not XRP-specific volume**, not a single-period flow, and not proof of XRP purchases. The XRP share remains undisclosed.

## XRP route

SBI reports an XRP-utilizing remittance service through SBI Remit beginning in 2021 and expansion to the Philippines, Vietnam and Indonesia in 2023. SBI Ripple Asia is a separate entity; SBI Group reports 60% voting rights in it as of March 31, 2026. This is distinct from SBI Group's approximately 9% shareholding in Ripple Labs reported in 2025/2026.

## New Fasset rail

On June 18, 2026, SBI Remit and Fasset signed an MoU to develop next-generation international remittance infrastructure using stablecoin payment technology. SBI's release quotes Fasset as reporting $32B annual transaction volume, 2M+ digital wallets, 16 blockchain networks and payment routes across 50+ banks. These are Fasset-reported claims, not independently verified here. The MoU does not disclose production launch, specific stablecoin issuers, chains, corridors or whether XRP will be used.

SBI Remit also added JPY bank-account remittances in June 2026, alongside local currencies, USD and EUR.

## Competing interpretations

1. Multi-rail strategy: SBI retains XRP-based corridors while adding stablecoin and fiat routes.
2. Substitution: Fasset stablecoins could replace XRP bridge legs in some corridors.
3. Complementarity: Fasset and XRP may serve different routes, counterparties or liquidity conditions.

The evidence does not yet choose among them. The key missing variable is asset-specific corridor volume.
