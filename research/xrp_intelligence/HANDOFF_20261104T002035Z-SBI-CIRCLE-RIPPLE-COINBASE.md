# SBI–Circle–Ripple–Coinbase relationship graph — 2026-11-04

## Main finding

SBI Group has documented relationships with both Ripple/XRP and Circle/USDC. It is not an either/or position in the official SBI materials:

- SBI says it invested in Ripple in 2016 and held approximately 9% of Ripple shares as of its June 2025 statement; it identifies SBI Ripple Asia as a joint venture and SBI Remit as offering international transfers using XRP.
- SBI and Circle signed an MOU in November 2023; they formed a 50/50 Japanese JV in 2025. SBI's investor presentation labels the same JV **Circle SBI Japan KK** in English and **SBI Circle Holdings K.K.** in Japanese.
- SBI Holdings and SBI Shinsei Bank acquired $50M of Circle shares at Circle's June 2025 IPO ($25M each).
- SBI VC Trade launched USDC for public customers in March 2025 and began handling RLUSD in June 2026.
- SBI announced participation through a consolidated subsidiary in Circle's ARC token presale in May 2026; the SBI subscription amount is undisclosed.

This is strong evidence of a multi-rail strategy. It does not prove that SBI is replacing XRP with USDC or that stablecoin adoption creates incremental XRP demand.

## Critical distinction: two meanings that can be confused

1. **Japanese regional SBIC companies:** Tokyo SBIC East Japan, Osaka SBIC West Japan and Nagoya SBIC Central Japan are separate SME investment/consultation companies.
2. **SBI Circle Holdings / Circle SBI Japan K.K.:** a separate 50/50 SBI-Circle JV for USDC distribution in Japan. The official SBI investor presentation explicitly gives the English and Japanese names for the same JV.

No official source reviewed uses “SBIC” as the formal acronym for the SBI-Circle JV. The user's phrase may be a shorthand or conflation; do not assume.

Tokyo SBIC's ChatGPT training is educational activity, not an OpenAI partnership.

## Circle JV operating status

Circle's Q2 2026 SEC 10-Q says Circle and SBI each contributed approximately $1.5M in November 2025; Circle owns 50%, consolidates the VIE because it controls relevant activities, and reported no significant operating results to date as of June 30, 2026. Equal equity ownership does not by itself establish equal governance rights. This is separate from SBI VC Trade's actual USDC distribution.

## Coinbase–Circle economics

Circle's Q2 2026 SEC 10-Q describes the distribution formula: Coinbase receives allocations tied to USDC held on its platform after issuer retention, plus half of the remaining broader ecosystem-growth payment base after approved third-party participant payments. Circle reported $324.6M in Q2 2026 and $655.3M in H1 2026 distribution costs in connection with agreements with Coinbase. These are accounting expenses, not a separately disclosed cash transfer.

Coinbase's Q3 2026 deck reports $22B average USDC held in Coinbase products, about 30% of average USDC market cap; the $22B includes $2B corporate balances. Coinbase also says it captured approximately 50% of USDC economics over the prior year. That is a company-defined claim, not an independently decomposed share of all Circle profit.

## XRP interpretation

- SBI's XRP remittance and Ripple equity/JV relationships are real corporate links to XRP.
- SBI's RLUSD and USDC distribution creates a stablecoin alternative and/or complement.
- SBI's Circle/Arc participation expands the relationship beyond USDC distribution.
- Coinbase's large USDC distribution economics can strengthen USDC market structure, but do not establish a negative causal effect on XRP.
- Coinbase XRP collateral eligibility remains separate from Circle's USDC distribution economics; actual XRP collateral utilization is unknown.

## Competing hypotheses

1. Multi-rail strategy: XRP for selected bridge/remittance use cases; USDC/RLUSD for dollar settlement and regulated stablecoin distribution.
2. Substitution: stablecoins displace XRP bridge legs in some corridors.
3. Complementarity: stablecoins provide stable value while XRP remains a bridge asset or network utility asset.
4. Circle/Arc expansion may create a competing settlement network, but no evidence shows SBI is abandoning XRPL.
5. Coinbase's USDC economics could shift liquidity toward USDC pairs, but no direct XRP price effect is established.
