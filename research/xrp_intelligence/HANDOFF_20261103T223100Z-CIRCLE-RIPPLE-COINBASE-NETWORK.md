## Bank charter comparison
- Circle's First National Digital Currency Bank, N.A. received final OCC approval July 9, 2026 and opened July 24. Initial scope was fiduciary custody for Circle and affiliates; Circle's Q2 filing says reserve management was not yet offered at opening. Circle Internet Financial remains USDC issuer.
- Ripple announced final OCC approval for Ripple National Trust Bank on October 26; OCC's CAS records consummated/effective October 26. Planned scope includes RLUSD reserve management, collateral trustee and fiduciary digital-asset custody. RLUSD remains issued by Standard Custody & Trust Company. The bank's first operating date was not verified.
- Coinbase National Trust Company had preliminary conditional approval dated April 2, 2026; no effective date was found in the reviewed OCC record.

These are national trust banks, not ordinary FDIC-insured deposit banks. Do not equate charter approval with XRP demand.

## Coinbase/Circle distribution economics
Circle's Q2 2026 SEC filing reports $324.6M of distribution costs paid to Coinbase in Q2 and $655.3M in H1 under USDC collaboration agreements. This is actual reported expense/distribution economics. Ripple's RLUSD distribution agreement with Coinbase is confirmed, but equivalent revenue-sharing terms and actual RLUSD volumes are not disclosed.

## Ripple's current institutional network
Ripple states RLUSD is already used as collateral within Ripple Prime and in on-chain repo markets; notional, haircuts and borrower mix are not disclosed. Coinbase Prime/Exchange/Retail distribution is announced, but Global Exchange collateral support was described as future. BNY is named primary reserve custodian and supports XRP/RLUSD custody. C2FO Instant Pay uses RLUSD on XRPL; named capital providers and throughput remain unknown. Ripple/Robinhood adds RLUSD on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp plus institutional settlement on XRPL; asset-level XRP volume remains unknown.

Ripple's $900M annualized product revenue run-rate, 1,700+ institutions, 60+ payout markets and $150B lifetime volume are company-reported aggregate metrics, not audited XRP-specific activity.

## SBI Group network
SBI Holdings has a 50:50 Circle SBI Japan JV for USDC distribution and separately distributes RLUSD through SBI VC Trade. It also has public ChatGPT Enterprise and Anthropic/Claude relationships. These are SBI Group links, not SBIC East Japan and not Barrot integrations. Relative USDC/RLUSD volumes and XRP bridge usage are unknown.

## Competitive interpretation
Coinbase is a multi-stablecoin distributor with material USDC revenue-sharing economics, a new RLUSD distribution relationship, XRP futures access through Nodal Clear, a CFTC FCM and a still-conditional trust-bank application. Circle and Ripple both have final federal trust-bank approvals recorded in 2026; they compete in stablecoins, custody and reserve services. No direct Circle-Ripple commercial partnership was established. They share counterparties including Coinbase, SBI Group and BNY.

None of these relationships alone proves incremental XRP spot demand. Require XRP-specific collateral balances, actual bridge flows, issuer-level ETF creations/redemptions, and attributable order-book/settlement evidence.
