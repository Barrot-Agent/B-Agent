# Evernorth actual-capital reconciliation and Japan SBIC identity

**Research captured:** 2026-11-27 UTC  
**Purpose:** Separate cash actually received, in-kind XRP contributions, debt, optional financing capacity and voting/economic ownership. Resolve the overloaded Japanese “SBIC” label from first-party evidence.  
**Scope:** Public SEC filings and official company pages only. This is a research record, not an XRP forecast or investment recommendation.

## Executive findings

1. **“SBIC East/Central/West” identifies three distinct regional statutory companies**: Tokyo, Nagoya and Osaka Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation companies. Their official profiles list one another as sister companies. They are not SBI Holdings, and they are not the SBI-controlled entities named in Evernorth's 2026 filings.
2. **SBI Holdings historically used “SBIC” for SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd.**, a separate buyout/enterprise-revitalization fund manager. SBI's 2007 release records a 60% SBI Holdings / 40% Goldman Sachs group ownership split after a share transfer; SBI's 2011 annual report later lists SBI CAPITAL as 100% owned by SBI Holdings. The transaction that restored 100% ownership and the entity's present 2026 corporate status were not established in this research.
3. **Evernorth's 2026 SBI investors are four named SBI-controlled entities**, not the regional SBIC companies: SBI Venture Fund 2023A Investment LPS, SBI Venture Fund 2023B Investment LPS, SBI PE Holdings Co., Ltd., and SBI Holdings USA, Inc. The SEC S-4/A says each is controlled by SBI Holdings, Inc. A 39.9% cap applies to the SBI subscriber group's Class A voting shares; excess subscription shares are Class C, which have economic rights but no ordinary voting rights and are convertible to Class A under the charter.
4. **Evernorth's capital must not be collapsed into one headline number.** The filings disclose gross cash at the October 9, 2026 closing, in-kind XRP, a $30m PIK note, cash warrant exercises and an optional equity line. These have different legal/economic meanings. The optional ELOC is not cash already raised.
5. The exact searches for **“ASK Coin”** and **“SBIC, ChatGPT SBIC”** produced no credible primary-source match tying those phrases to Ripple, XRP or the named SBI entities. “Actual capital” is best resolved by the SEC cash-flow and transaction disclosures below, not by secondary commentary.
6. A public YouTube channel self-identifying as **Dr Kamilah Stevenson** is a candidate identity, but the available first-party cross-link is not reciprocal and a second similarly named channel exists. “Camilla” and “Kamilah” are not merged; no personal-video inventory is authorized by this evidence threshold yet.

## 2. SBI Holdings' separate historical use of “SBIC”

SBI Holdings' official English release dated **21 May 2007** describes a transfer of 40% of **SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd.** to Jupiter Investment Y.K., a Goldman Sachs group company. The post-transfer ownership was **60% SBI Holdings / 40% Jupiter Investment Y.K.** The business was buyout and enterprise-revitalization fund management. The release uses “SBIC” in describing the stock transfer.

SBI Holdings' official **2011 annual report** subsequently lists **SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd. at 100.0% ownership** within the Asset Management Business. This establishes that the company was again listed as wholly owned by SBI Holdings by that reporting period; the intervening share-transfer history was not resolved here.

This historical SBI Capital is **not** Tokyo SBIC, Nagoya SBIC or Osaka SBIC. Similar abbreviation, different legal entities and functions.

Primary sources:
- [SBI Holdings — 21 May 2007 English release (PDF)](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/english/news/pdf/2007/0521_a_en.pdf).
- [SBI Holdings — 2011 Annual Report](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/english/investors/library/annualreport/pdf/2011ar_e-all.pdf).

**Current-status limitation:** The 2011 ownership record is historical. This review does not establish whether SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd. retained the same name, was reorganized or was merged after 2011. Do not treat the 2007 “SBIC” label as a current corporate identity without a current registry or SBI group-company filing.

## 3. Which SBI entities are actually named in Evernorth's transaction

The SEC Form S-4/A filed **12 August 2026** names four SBI Stockholders:

- SBI Venture Fund 2023A Investment LPS
- SBI Venture Fund 2023B Investment LPS
- SBI PE Holdings Co., Ltd.
- SBI Holdings USA, Inc.

The filing expressly says each is controlled by **SBI Holdings, Inc.** The SBI Entity Amendments apply a **39.9% ownership limitation** to the shares of Evernorth Class A common stock issuable in respect of SBI's Advance Funding Subscription. At closing, the SBI subscriber group receives Class A shares up to the 39.9% voting-ownership threshold; the balance is issued as Class C. Class C has economic rights but no ordinary voting rights and is convertible into Class A under the charter.

The 10-Q repeats that certain investors affiliated with SBI Holdings amended their Advance Funding Subscription Agreements to impose the 39.9% Class A threshold. It does **not** identify the regional SBIC companies as investors. It also does not disclose the individual cash amount attributable to each of the four SBI entities. Therefore, do not attribute the entire $214.05m Advance Funding total to SBI.

Primary source:
- [SEC S-4/A, filed 12 August 2026, accession 000119312526347519](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092032/000119312526347519/d944435ds4a.htm), especially the SBI Entity Amendments and Class A/Class C terms.
- [SEC Evernorth Form 10-Q, quarter ended 30 September 2026, accession 000162828026073871](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000162828026073871/xrpn-20260930.htm), Note 3 / subsequent-event disclosures.

## 4. Evernorth cash, XRP and financing ledger

### 4.1 Advance and delayed subscriptions

The 10-Q and S-4/A disclose:

- Advance Funding subscribers paid **$214,049,995 cash by 23 October 2025**. Evernorth used that cash to purchase **84,365,876 XRP**, received in tranches by 6 November 2025.
- Advance Funding subscribers also contributed **600,000 XRP in kind**.
- Delayed Funding subscribers committed **$10.5m cash plus 200,000 XRP**, due at closing.
- The S-4/A states that the aggregate Advance Funding subscription included multiple institutional and accredited investors. SBI-controlled entities were among the subscribers, but the reviewed filing excerpt does not allocate the $214.05m among them.

The $214.05m was actual cash received in 2025, but it was subsequently deployed into XRP. It is not an unspent cash reserve.

### 4.2 Other XRP contributions

- **Ripple:** contributed **126,791,458 XRP** to Pathfinder Digital Assets LLC under the Contribution Agreement. The SEC describes this as XRP contributed in exchange for Pathfinder units, not cash. Ripple's and its affiliated Contributor Related Party Entity's combined Class A ownership was capped at 9.9%; excess interests were represented by Pathfinder units.
- **RippleWorks / Arrington sponsor:** RippleWorks contributed **211,319,096.061435 XRP** to Arrington XRP Capital Fund, LP, subject to the sponsor investing the XRP for Evernorth shares. The sponsor's voting Class A ownership was capped at 19.9%; excess subscribed shares were Class C.
- **Ripple-affiliated Contributor Related Party Entity:** transferred **50,000,000 XRP** on 20 October 2025 into escrow for the transaction; the resulting equity/Pathfinder-unit allocation was subject to the combined Ripple / related-party 9.9% Class A limit.
- **Delayed Funding:** included a further **200,000 XRP** in kind at closing.

The in-kind XRP amounts are not cash proceeds. They are also not additive to later custody/transfer balances without reconciling the same XRP through the transaction chain.

### 4.3 October 9, 2026 closing

The 10-Q states that, in connection with closing, Evernorth received approximately:

- **$272.6m gross cash**, comprising approximately **$224.6m from Private Placement Investments** and **$48.0m released from the SPAC trust account after redemptions**, before transaction expenses.
- Approximately **388,910,554 XRP contributed in kind**.

The 388.910554m XRP is an asset contribution, not cash. It includes the separate in-kind investor/Ripple-related contributions; it must not be added to the 346.484972m post-close transfer below as if the two were independent pools.

The 10-Q's pre-close balance sheet is explicitly historical and does not represent the post-close public-company cash position.

### 4.4 XRP custody / transfer bridge

The 10-Q reports approximately **346,284,972 XRP held by Evernorth as of 30 September 2026**. The November 13 subsequent-events disclosure says Evernorth subsequently transferred **346,484,972 XRP to Pathfinder** in connection with the business combination. The 200,000 XRP difference matches the delayed XRP subscription.

Separately, Ripple had contributed **126,791,458 XRP to Pathfinder**. The filings therefore disclose two distinct components at different transaction stages. A mechanical sum would be **473,276,430 XRP**, but this is **not certified as a current post-close balance**: the cited disclosures do not provide a post-close Pathfinder custody statement or a complete subsequent sale/use ledger. Do not use the mechanical sum as a current treasury balance.

### 4.5 Convertible note: debt, not equity proceeds

Evernorth agreed to issue **$30.0m aggregate principal** of 4.00% Convertible Senior PIK Notes due 2031 to NH Investment & Securities as trustee for Kyobo AIM Corporate Finance General Private Investment Trust No. 3.

- The post-effective S-1/A states issuance on **12 October 2026** for $30.0m cash consideration.
- The November 13 424B3 sticker states issuance on **13 October 2026** and **$29.0m net cash proceeds after a $1.0m advisor fee**.
- These two filings differ by one calendar day on the issuance/effectiveness date. Preserve the discrepancy; do not silently select one.
- The notes accrue 4.00% PIK interest, mature in 2031 and have conversion rights. A holder put following an event of default or fundamental transaction can produce an 8.00% yield-to-put. This is debt financing, not equity and not free capital.

Primary sources:
- [SEC Post-Effective Amendment No. 1, accession 000119312526420749](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000119312526420749/d162844dposam.htm).

### 4.6 Warrant proceeds

The 10-Q reports that, between **12 October and 9 November 2026**, holders exercised **3,073,366 warrants for cash**, producing **$35.3m gross proceeds**. **8,666,279 warrants remained outstanding** after those exercises. Each warrant has an $11.50 exercise price. Remaining warrants are potential future proceeds only if exercised; they are not cash already received.

Primary source: [SEC 424B3 sticker / 10-Q subsequent events, accession 000162828026073926](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000162828026073926/evernorth-424stickerresalea.htm).

### 4.7 ELOC: optional share issuance, not committed cash

On 23 October 2026, Evernorth entered a common-stock purchase agreement with CF Principal Investments LLC (Cantor), allowing Evernorth, at its sole discretion, to direct purchases of up to **5,218,935 Class A shares**. Purchases are priced at **97% of applicable VWAP**, are subject to volume and ownership caps, and require an effective registration statement and other conditions. The registration statement became effective on 30 October 2026. Evernorth paid a **$0.5m non-refundable commitment fee**.

The facility does **not** mean $5.2m, $50m or any other fixed amount of cash has been raised. Proceeds depend on actual draw notices, purchase prices and volumes. The $0.5m fee is a cash outflow.

Primary source: [SEC Evernorth Form 10-Q, ELOC section](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2092592/000162828026073871/xrpn-20260930.htm).

### 4.8 Pre-close liquidity and accounting caveat

- Unrestricted cash: **$91,397** (cash plus restricted cash was $141,392).
- Net working-capital deficit: **$10.0m**.
- Accumulated deficit: **$185.6m**.
- Nine-month operating loss: **$273.2m**, including **$271.0m digital-asset impairment**.

## 7. Coinbase, Circle, Ripple, SBI and custody — issuer-specific relationship map

The same SEC filings provide a useful separation of market reference, custody, ownership and stablecoin context:

- **Coinbase — principal-market price reference, not the disclosed custodian.** Evernorth's 10-Q says management selected Coinbase as the principal market for XRP fair-value accounting after considering market-based volume/activity, quote consistency and regulatory/AML/KYC compliance. The 10-Q uses Coinbase's September 30, 2026 close of **$1.49** for its XRP fair-value measurement. The S-4/A's June 30, 2026 illustrative valuation uses **$1.04** reported by Coinbase. These are date-specific accounting inputs, not current prices, proof of exclusive liquidity, or evidence Evernorth actively traded through Coinbase. The 10-Q says Evernorth did not actively trade XRP on digital-asset markets.
- **BitGo Trust — primary disclosed custodian.** The S-4/A says Evernorth holds its XRP through BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A. (formerly BitGo Trust Company), under custody agreements. As of the prospectus date, it states 100% of XRP was in cold storage. The described approximately **$250m insurance** is an aggregate policy shared among BitGo clients, not coverage dedicated to Evernorth; the filing estimates that it would cover only a fraction of expected holdings under its illustrative assumptions. Therefore do not conflate Coinbase's principal-market role with custody.
- **Circle — USDC context, not a disclosed Evernorth counterparty.** The S-4/A mentions USDC as a reserve-backed stablecoin issued by Circle and commonly used in digital-asset markets, including the XRP market. It cites the March 2023 depeg after Circle disclosed $3.3bn of reserves at Silicon Valley Bank. In the reviewed Evernorth 10-Q and S-4/A, no direct Circle investment, custody, settlement, issuer, or commercial agreement with Evernorth is disclosed. This is an absence in these filings, not proof that no Circle relationship exists elsewhere.
- **Ripple — pre-close parent, contributor and related-party lender.** Before the October 9, 2026 closing, Evernorth was wholly owned by Ripple. After the combination, Evernorth ceased to be wholly owned or consolidated by Ripple and describes Ripple as a related party. Ripple contributed 126,791,458 XRP to Pathfinder; a Ripple-affiliated entity contributed 50m XRP; Ripple provided a $1m revolving facility at 3.81%, which Evernorth had not drawn as of September 30, 2026. Pathfinder also funded certain operating costs, creating a related-party payable.
- **SBI — investor group, not regional SBIC.** The four SBI-controlled entities and 39.9% Class A voting cap are described above. The SEC filings do not identify Tokyo/Nagoya/Osaka SBIC as subscribers or counterparties.
- **No inferred bilateral settlement:** Coinbase's valuation role, BitGo custody, Circle's USDC risk disclosure, Ripple's XRP contribution and SBI's equity subscription are different relationships. None by itself proves that Coinbase or Circle settled Evernorth's XRP transactions, that USDC was used to acquire the contributed XRP, or that regional SBIC companies participated in the transaction.
