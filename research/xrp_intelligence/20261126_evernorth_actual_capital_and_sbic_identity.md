## 2. SBI Holdings' separate historical use of “SBIC”

SBI Holdings' official English release dated **21 May 2007** describes a transfer of 40% of **SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd.** to Jupiter Investment Y.K., a Goldman Sachs group company. The post-transfer ownership was **60% SBI Holdings / 40% Jupiter Investment Y.K.** The business was buyout and enterprise-revitalization fund management. The release uses “SBIC” in describing the stock transfer.

SBI Holdings' official **2011 annual report** subsequently lists **SBI CAPITAL Co., Ltd. at 100.0% ownership** within the Asset Management Business. This establishes that the company was again listed as wholly owned by SBI Holdings by that reporting period; the intervening share-transfer history was not resolved here.

This historical SBI Capital is **not** Tokyo SBIC, Nagoya SBIC or Osaka SBIC. Similar abbreviation, different legal entities and functions.
