# SBIC / ChatGPT SBIC / ASK Coin disambiguation — 2026-11-03 15:25 UTC

## SBIC has two verified meanings in the current evidence
1. **Japan:** SBIC East Japan is Tokyo Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation Co., Ltd. Its official profile reports establishment on 15 November 1963, 96 employees, ¥6.6734B capital and an 18-prefecture operating area. The company says it is an independent private corporation and policy-implementing institution under Japan's 1963 SME Investment & Consultation Companies Act. It is not SBI Holdings.
2. **Academic:** SBIC also means **Social Bias Inference Corpus**, a dataset described in the 2019 Social Bias Frames paper. The 2024 paper *Efficient Toxic Content Detection by Bootstrapping and Distilling Large Language Models* explicitly evaluates ChatGPT (gpt-3.5-turbo) on SBIC, using 4,000 training and 1,000 test examples. It reports 68.5% accuracy / 72.82 F1 for the ChatGPT CoT baseline and 72.0% / 74.91 F1 for its distilled method.

The exact phrase **“ChatGPT SBIC”** therefore has a strong, direct academic-context match. It does not indicate a Japanese corporate partnership, an OpenAI partnership with the Japanese SBIC company, or an XRP relationship.
