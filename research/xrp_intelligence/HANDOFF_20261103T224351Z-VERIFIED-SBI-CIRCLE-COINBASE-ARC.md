## Strategic interpretation

SBI is best described as multi-rail: historical Ripple equity and XRP remittance ties coexist with Circle equity, a 50:50 USDC JV, USDC distribution, JPYSC development and a founding-validator role on Circle Arc. Arc may compete with XRPL for institutional stablecoin and agentic settlement, but displacement of Ripple/XRP is not established.

Coinbase is a distribution and market-infrastructure hub spanning Circle USDC, Ripple RLUSD and XRP derivatives collateral. The economics and actual asset utilization are asymmetric and incompletely disclosed.

Stablecoin settlement on XRPL does not automatically create XRP demand. C2FO specifies RLUSD settlement; Circle Arc uses USDC for gas. XRP bridge or collateral demand must be measured directly.
