## New institutional findings
1. Circle and Binance announced a five-year USDC commercial agreement and Binance's $100M strategic equity investment in Circle on September 22. Binance promotes/integrates USDC; Circle provides infrastructure. This is a direct Circle-Binance relationship, not proof of Circle influence over XRP prices.
2. Coinbase renewed its Circle arrangement on the same terms. Coinbase reported Q3 stablecoin revenue of $306.905M, down from $324.534M year over year, while average USDC balances in its products reached about $22B, up 49%. Higher balances and lower reserve rates have opposing revenue effects.
3. SBI VC Trade and Ripple launched RLUSD in Japan on June 24 after JFSA approval. This is RLUSD distribution; it must not be conflated with native XRP payment demand.
4. JPYSC is issued by SBI Shinsei Trust Bank and distributed by SBI VC Trade. It began as an account-limited offering; public-chain circulation was conditional on legal and tax-practice readiness.
5. SBI VC Trade reported on November 2 that JPYSC lending balances exceeded ¥10B and handled balance exceeded ¥30B. The handled balance includes on-chain plus lending balances. It is not freely transferable public-chain circulation or a reserve figure.
6. JPYSC lending courses on November 2 were 28 days at 2%, 84 days at 2.5%, and 168 days at 3% annualized. Early termination is generally unavailable; the service is not a bank deposit or deposit-insured.
7. SBI's September 7 disclosure reported ¥1B of JPYSC trust backing invested in short-term Japanese government bills, with issuance around ¥20.1B and lending balances around ¥6.9B at that date.
8. SBI and SMFG's October 30 alliance includes SMFG's 5% participation in SBI Startale Marketing. The stated work is research, use-case evaluation and potential proofs-of-concept.
9. SBI, Startale and BlackRock's October 27 MOU explores tokenized MMF and JPYSC linkage; it is not a live production integration.
10. Tokyo SBIC is Tokyo Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation Co., not SBI Holdings. Its February 2025 ChatGPT seminar was co-hosted with the Taito Chamber of Commerce, free for investee and general companies, and featured a Microsoft speaker. No OpenAI partnership or XRP integration is established.
