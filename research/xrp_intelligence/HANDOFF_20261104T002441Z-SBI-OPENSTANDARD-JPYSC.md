# SBI–Startale–Open Standard–JPYSC network update — 2026-11-04

## Main finding

SBI's Japan strategy now spans more than Ripple/XRP and Circle/USDC:

- **JPYSC:** issued by SBI Shinsei Trust Bank and distributed by SBI VC Trade, co-developed with Startale. Initial June 24, 2026 service was account-internal; public-chain distribution was deferred pending legal/tax readiness.
- **Open Standard/OUSD:** SBI and Startale joined on October 16 and are exploring JPYSC–OUSD interoperability. The official OUSD partner page lists SBI Holdings and Coinbase among a 200+ partner network.
- **BlackRock:** SBI and Startale signed an October 27 MoU to explore linking tokenized money-market funds and JPYSC.
- **SMBC:** on October 30, SMFG agreed to take 5% of SBI Startale Marketing, the SBI/Startale JV promoting JPYSC. The investment amount and governance terms were not disclosed.
- **Startale:** SBI announced an approximately $50M investment contract in March 2026; closing/payment not independently verified.

This supports a multi-rail strategy hypothesis. It does not establish that SBI is abandoning XRP or that the new stablecoins have displaced XRP bridge legs.

## OUSD: current reserve evidence and a data-quality conflict

Bridge's issuer transparency dashboard, last updated **2026-11-04 00:00 UTC**, shows:

- Supply: **$888,329,666**
- Reserves: **$890,329,784**
- Overcollateralization: **$2,000,117**
- Reserve ratio: **100.23%**
- Cash: **$115,102,996 (13%)**
- Treasury: **$773,226,672 (87%)**, including money-market funds with T-bill ladders under three months.

Bridge says monthly independent attestations are provided, but the page showed only August 2026 as an available attestation at capture.

The OpenUSD marketing page simultaneously reports **$468M** circulation and a 54.9% cash / 45.1% short-Treasury mix as of October. It also says OUSD is live while displaying a future November 12 public-launch event. Stripe separately announced business availability on September 30. Preserve the inconsistency; do not average or assume one scope.

## JPYSC actual capital and collateral

SBI's September 7 disclosure reports approximately ¥20.1B JPYSC outstanding, ¥6.9B in lending applications, and ¥1B of trust assets allocated to short-term Japanese government bonds. Lending applications are not necessarily funded loans; the combined ¥27B figure is not circulating supply.

## Strategic implications

Coinbase is a founding partner/investor in Open Standard while continuing to distribute Circle's USDC and reporting substantial USDC balances. OUSD's partner-income model may change the distribution economics of dollar stablecoins. SBI is a partner in that same network while exploring JPYSC/OUSD interoperability.

The most useful competing hypotheses are:
1. SBI is diversifying across multiple settlement rails.
2. JPYSC/OUSD could substitute for XRP bridge use in some yen-dollar corridors.
3. Stablecoins could complement XRP by handling fiat-denominated settlement while XRP supplies bridge liquidity or network utility.
4. Coinbase is hedging its stablecoin position by retaining USDC economics while co-founding OUSD.

No public route-level data yet establishes which hypothesis dominates.
