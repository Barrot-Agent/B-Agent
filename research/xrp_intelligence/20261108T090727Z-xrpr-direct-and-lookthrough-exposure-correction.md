## Classification correction

XRP Insights calls XRPR one of six US spot XRP ETFs and reports **33.4M XRP via CoinShares**. That is a look-through estimate, not 33.4M XRP directly custodied by REX. The better product classification is five direct XRP spot/ETP products, one mixed direct/indirect fund (XRPR), and one multi-asset index product (BITW).

The difference between XRP Insights' 33.4M look-through estimate and REX's 19.885M direct XRP is 13.515M XRP-equivalent. Dividing by 362,750 CoinShares ETP shares gives an implied 37.256 XRP-equivalent per share, but the underlying CoinShares ratio was not independently verified.

## Aggregate reconciliation hypothesis

CryptoKeyhole's four daily direct-fund holdings (1,076,805,885 XRP) + Grayscale's Nov 6 primary issuer units (64,838,291.3) + XRP Insights' REX look-through estimate (33.4M) + BITW estimate (20.9M) = 1,195,944,176.3 XRP.

XRP Insights' undated dashboard reports 1,195,180,000 XRP, a difference of 764,176 XRP (0.064%). The arithmetic is close, but the dates and look-through methods are mixed; it is not a verified common-cutoff total.

CryptoKeyhole excludes XRPR from its daily flow universe because its daily file reports direct coins but not total look-through exposure. This is a defensible methodology distinction, not proof that XRPR has no XRP exposure.
