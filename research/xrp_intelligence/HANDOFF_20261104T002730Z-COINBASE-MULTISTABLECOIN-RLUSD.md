# Coinbase multi-stablecoin distribution and RLUSD listing — 2026-11-04

## Coinbase's current position

Coinbase is no longer accurately described as a USDC-only distribution venue:

- **USDC/Circle:** Coinbase remains a major USDC distribution partner. Circle's Q2 2026 SEC filing reports $324.6M Q2 and $655.3M H1 distribution costs connected with Coinbase agreements. Coinbase's Q3 deck reports $22B average USDC held in Coinbase products; $2B of that is corporate balances. Coinbase says it captured approximately 50% of USDC economics over the prior year.
- **OUSD/Open Standard:** Coinbase is one of five founding partners of Open Standard and a participant in a new partner-revenue stablecoin model. OUSD is issued by Bridge, a Stripe company.
- **RLUSD/Ripple:** Coinbase's October 28 official announcement added RLUSD to Coinbase Prime, Coinbase Exchange and the retail app in supported regions. Deposits/withdrawals are supported across XRP Ledger, Base and Ethereum; USD-to-RLUSD conversion is 1:1. Ripple said it would provide liquidity on Base.
- **Important boundary:** Coinbase explicitly said RLUSD availability on Coinbase Global Exchange, including use as collateral, would follow. Do not treat RLUSD as already eligible collateral there at launch.

This is evidence of a multi-stablecoin platform strategy: Coinbase can monetize custody, conversion, trading, settlement, collateral and distribution across issuers while retaining a major USDC relationship.

## XRP connection

- XRP is already eligible collateral for selected Coinbase Global Exchange perpetual products.
- cbXRP is a Base-wrapped representation backed 1:1 by XRP held by Coinbase; the Nov 4 00:17 displayed snapshot showed 152.206M XRP reserves versus 152.203M cbXRP supply.
- Coinbase also offers USDC borrowing through Morpho on Base with XRP collateral; New York is excluded from stated US availability.
- XRP-PERP is USDC-settled. The collateral asset and settlement asset are distinct.
- RLUSD transfers are supported on XRPL, but stablecoin activity does not imply proportional XRP spot demand. XRP is used for network fees/reserves and may be used as a bridge in some routes; route-level evidence is needed.

## Competing interpretations

1. Coinbase is a multi-issuer distribution/custody platform, not a single-stablecoin champion.
2. RLUSD on XRPL may increase ledger activity while creating little direct XRP spot demand.
3. OUSD could pressure Circle's distribution economics, but no USDC displacement is yet established.
4. XRP collateral and cbXRP increase capital utility but may add liquidation feedback; actual utilization remains unknown.
