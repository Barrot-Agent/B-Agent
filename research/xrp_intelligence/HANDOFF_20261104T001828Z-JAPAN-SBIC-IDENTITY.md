# Japan SBIC identity resolution — 2026-11-04

## Correction

The earlier single-entity framing was too narrow. Official records identify three separate regional sister companies that use SBIC branding:

- **SBIC East Japan:** Tokyo Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation Co., Ltd.; established 15 November 1963; corporate number 9011001030621.
- **SBIC West Japan:** Osaka Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation Co., Ltd.; established 20 November 1963.
- **SBIC Central Japan:** Nagoya Small and Medium Business Investment & Consultation Co., Ltd.; established 18 November 1963.

Their official profiles cross-list one another as sister companies. They operate under Japan's SME Investment & Consultation Companies Act framework. Therefore, "Japan's SBIC" is ambiguous unless the originating source identifies the regional company.

## ChatGPT connection

Tokyo SBIC's official June 12–13, 2025 training curriculum explicitly included ChatGPT usage and generative-AI education. Its official articles also discuss ChatGPT/GPT-5. This proves education and editorial coverage, not an OpenAI partnership, integration, financing relationship or shared project.

## XRP / Ripple / SBI boundary
