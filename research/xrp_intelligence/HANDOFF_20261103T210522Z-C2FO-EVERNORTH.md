C2FO and Ripple announced on 2026-10-27 that C2FO Instant Pay financing and settlement activity is intended to use XRPL with RLUSD as the settlement asset. Participating capital providers fund accelerated supplier payments; suppliers receive funds in seconds against unchanged invoice terms. This is a concrete XRPL/RLUSD enterprise use case, but not yet evidence of material XRP token demand.

## Material new finding: Evernorth / Cantor ELOC

SEC Notice of Effectiveness confirms Evernorth S-1 file 333-299620 became effective on October 30, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. ET. This clears the registration-statement condition, not all commencement/purchase conditions.

The agreement permits optional issuance/sale of up to 5,218,935 new shares at 97% of applicable VWAP, subject to a 20% ordinary daily-volume cap, 4.99% Cantor beneficial-ownership cap, price thresholds and other conditions.
