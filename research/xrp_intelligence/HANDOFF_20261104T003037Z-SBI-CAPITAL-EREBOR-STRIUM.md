# SBI capital classification, Erebor and Strium — 2026-11-04

## New $100M US banking investment

On November 2, 2026, SBI Holdings announced that its US subsidiary SBI Holdings USA would invest **$100M in Erebor Bank, N.A.** The announcement says the investment is intended to strengthen SBI's US business base and deepen its understanding of next-generation digital banking. SBI says Erebor supports blockchain-based 24/7 settlement and stablecoin deposits/withdrawals, and had deposits above $7B as of September 2026.

Erebor serves defense, AI, energy, robotics and advanced manufacturing companies. SBI did not name a specific Ripple, Circle, Coinbase, USDC, RLUSD or XRP integration. The investment is announced as a future investment; completion, ownership percentage and governance rights are not yet verified.

## Strium

SBI and Startale announced Strium on February 5, 2026: an institutional-focused L1 for tokenized securities, RWA-linked products and 24/7 spot/derivatives markets. The project was in proof-of-concept, with a testnet planned. No XRP/XRPL integration is named. It may become an alternative institutional RWA rail, but no migration or direct competition is established.

## Critical capital distinctions

- **SBI ~9% Ripple stake:** equity in Ripple Labs, not XRP tokens. A February 2026 public clarification by SBI Chairman Yoshitaka Kitao, reported by CryptoNews, rejected the claim that SBI held $10B of XRP; the 9% refers to Ripple equity. The direct X post was not independently retrieved in this run.
- **SBI/SBI Shinsei $50M Circle investment:** $25M each in Circle shares; separate from the SBI-Circle JV and USDC reserves.
- **SBI-Circle JV:** Circle SBI Japan K.K. / SBI Circle Holdings K.K.; 50/50 equity. Circle's SEC filing says each contributed approximately $1.5M in November 2025 and that the JV had no significant operating results to date as of June 30, 2026.
- **SBI Startale $50M:** announced investment contract; closing/payment not independently verified.
- **SBI $100M Erebor:** announced investment; completion not verified.
- **Circle ARC presale:** $242.2M gross proceeds, $222M received in Q2 2026 and $20.2M receivable; SBI's subsidiary participated, but its allocation is unknown.
- **SBI Remit ¥2.5T:** cumulative total remittance handling across all channels, not XRP-specific.
- **JPYSC ¥20.1B:** outstanding stablecoin supply; ¥6.9B lending applications are not necessarily funded loans.
- **Coinbase cbXRP ~152.2M XRP reserve:** custody backing for wrapped cbXRP, not proof that all reserves are pledged as collateral.

## Interpretation

SBI's strategy is broad: Ripple/XRP, Circle/USDC, RLUSD, JPYSC, OUSD, Strium, Fasset and US banking rails. This is consistent with multi-rail diversification. It does not establish a preference for one asset or prove that new stablecoin rails have displaced XRP.
