The filing does not identify XRP in either recognized lending or derivatives collateral. If XRP is included in the $9.903M aggregate other-crypto lending bucket, its recognized lending-collateral value cannot exceed that entire bucket; actual XRP may be zero. The $2.804112B unrecognized borrower crypto-collateral pool is not broken down by token.

The derivatives table lists BTC at $298.212M and ETH at $3.731M. No XRP line appears. This is a September 30 quarter-end snapshot, not proof of no XRP use after the October 1 Global Exchange policy update.

Coinbase's own $434.163M of assets pledged against short-term borrowings is a separate balance-sheet category and must not be confused with customer-pledged collateral.

Loan collateral requirements ranged from 100% to 250% of the fair value of the loan.

## Circle and stablecoin economics

Coinbase reported $306.905M Q3 stablecoin revenue versus $324.534M in Q3 2025. In Q1 2026 it reclassified revenue earned on corporate payment-stablecoin balances from Stablecoin revenue to Corporate interest and other income. Coinbase states the reclassification did not change its Circle arrangement or the revenue generated.

The 10-Q says Coinbase renewed its arrangement with Circle on the same terms and that average USDC held in Coinbase products reached an all-time high. The filing does not state the exact average USDC balance.

## Strategic position

Coinbase identifies three 2026 priorities:
1. Grow the everything exchange.
2. Scale stablecoins and payments.
3. Expand onchain adoption.

Q3 total revenue was $1.216B, net revenue $1.160B, transaction revenue $590.224M and subscription/services revenue $570.238M. Assets on platform were $376.6B versus $515.9B a year earlier; MTUs were 7.4M versus 9.3M. Coinbase attributes the AOP decline primarily to lower crypto-asset prices, so it is not a direct measure of customer outflows.

## Interpretation

XRP product eligibility is not proof of material XRP utilization. The Q3 filing predates the October 1 collateral-policy update. No XRP-specific pledged balance, loan balance or derivatives-collateral balance is disclosed. The aggregate categories must not be assigned to XRP.

The Coinbase-Circle relationship is economically important, but the Stablecoin revenue line alone understates the full corporate payment-stablecoin balance presentation after the Q1 2026 accounting reclassification.

## Next tests

- Seek post-October 1 and later-quarter XRP-specific collateral utilization disclosures.
- Reconcile Stablecoin revenue with Corporate interest and other income across filings.
- Keep product eligibility, actual collateral balances, USDC distribution and RLUSD distribution as separate relationship types.

No directional XRP forecast is issued.
