# SBI, Ripple, Circle and Coinbase — primary-source relationship map
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-11-04T21:20:00Z

## Verified direct relationships

- **SBI Holdings → Ripple:** SBI Holdings participated as a strategic investor in Ripple's $55 million Series B in September 2016. The release does not disclose SBI's individual investment or ownership percentage. Ripple and SBI also established **SBI Ripple Asia**; SBI's group profile reports 60% of its voting rights as of March 31, 2026.
- **SBI Remit / SBI VC Trade → XRP remittances:** the September 2023 operating release says SBI Remit expanded Ripple Payments using XRP to bank-account corridors in the Philippines, Vietnam and Indonesia. SBI VC Trade sends XRP in real time against SBI Remit requests. The release proves the operating design at launch, not current 2026 volume or XRP inventory.
- **SBI VC Trade → RLUSD:** after a 2025 MOU with Ripple subsidiary Standard Custody & Trust, SBI VC Trade began handling RLUSD on June 24, 2026. This is a live product-distribution announcement, not proof of volume or collateral use.
- **SBI Holdings → Circle:** direct relationship across a 2023 MOU, a 2025 SBI Circle Holdings joint venture, and a disclosed $50 million Circle share investment at IPO ($25 million by SBI Holdings and $25 million by SBI Shinsei Bank). SBI VC Trade began general USDC trading in March 2025. Circle later named SBI Group as an Arc founding validator.
- **SBI Ripple Asia → XRPL:** a February 2026 proof of concept examined tokenized deposits and deposit-backed digital money using SBI's MoneyTap infrastructure, SBI Ripple Asia's token issuance platform and XRPL. It is a conceptual proof, not a production bank token or evidence that XRP itself is the bridge asset.
- **Ripple → Coinbase:** the October 28, 2026 RLUSD partnership put RLUSD on Coinbase Prime, Exchange and Retail across XRPL, Base and Ethereum. Both official announcements say Global Exchange collateral availability would follow; do not mark it live without a later confirmation.
- **Coinbase → Circle:** prior SEC filing review documents USDC distribution economics and Coinbase's economic exposure to Circle. See the referenced Circle Q3 and Coinbase filing analysis in the earlier market/institutional update.

## Important non-relationships and distinctions

- No direct Coinbase–SBI bilateral agreement or equity relationship was established in the reviewed primary sources. Their indirect connection is through shared RLUSD/USDC product ecosystems and Ripple/Circle.
- No direct Ripple–Circle partnership was established in the reviewed sources. Both issuers separately connect to SBI and Coinbase.
- SBI Group's Arc validator role is on Circle's separate Arc L1, not XRPL validation.
- SBI Ripple Asia's XRPL proof of concept must not be conflated with XRP bridge-asset usage.
- SBIC East/Central/West are not SBI Holdings and are not SBI Ripple Asia.
- SBI Group's 60% SBI Ripple Asia voting interest is disclosed; the remaining 40% is not attributed here.
- Do not repeat an exact SBI Ripple equity percentage such as 9% without a current primary filing that states it.

## What matters for XRP intelligence

The SBI Remit corridor announcement is direct evidence of an XRP bridge-asset use case. RLUSD distribution through SBI VC Trade and Coinbase expands stablecoin access on XRPL, but stablecoin transfers do not automatically require XRP as the transfer asset. The tokenized-deposit proof of concept and Circle Arc validator role are infrastructure relationships, not XRP demand. Current XRP volumes, balances, custody and collateral amounts remain undisclosed in the reviewed sources.
