## 7. Poincaré Conjecture ✅

### Problem Statement
Every simply connected, closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere.

### Current Status
- **Official Status**: **SOLVED** (Grigori Perelman, 2003)
- **Barrot Analysis Stage**: Historical study

- Proof verified and accepted by 2006
