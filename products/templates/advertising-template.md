# Advertising and affiliate template

Paid placement and affiliate links are only honest when the reader can tell, before
clicking, that money changed hands.

## Title formula

`[Placement type] on [surface], [impressions or period], disclosure on every unit`
The title names the unit of sale. A slot is sold as a defined unit, never as
"exposure".

## Pricing table

| Unit | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter slot | 49-199 | one send, one link, labelled sponsored |
| Site banner | 29-99 per week | fixed placement, no rotation |
| Affiliate | 10-30 percent | tracked link, disclosed at the link |
| Bundle sponsor | 199-499 | one sponsor per issue, no category exclusivity claim |

## Outline

1. Inventory: what surfaces exist, with real numbers
2. Unit definitions and screenshots
3. Disclosure rules, exact wording, placement
4. Affiliate tracking and attribution window
5. Refusal categories: no gambling, no health claims, no trading signals
6. Reporting: impressions, clicks, conversions, refunds
7. Cancellation and make-good policy

## Deliverables checklist

- [ ] inventory sheet with measured traffic, not projected
- [ ] disclosure sentence placed before the link
- [ ] affiliate tracking method documented
- [ ] refusal list
- [ ] monthly report template with definitions
- [ ] sponsor approval step, no automatic insertion
- [ ] policy for ads that underperform

## Sales page copy structure

For sponsors:
1. Headline: the audience, with a measured number
2. Units and prices
3. Disclosure policy
4. Reporting example
5. Book a slot

For readers:
1. One sentence explaining that sponsored units are labelled
2. Link to the full policy
