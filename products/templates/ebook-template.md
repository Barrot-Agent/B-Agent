# Ebook template

## Title formula

`The [Named Method] Playbook: [Outcome] in [Time or Count]`
The title must survive the question "what changes for the reader today". A subtitle
carries the audience and the promise. 30-80 pages is a constraint on scope, not a
target to pad.

## Pricing table

| Tier | Price | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| PDF | 19-29 | ebook, one format |
| PDF + templates | 39-59 | ebook plus the worksheets it references |
| Cohort | 149-299 | ebook plus one live review session |

## Outline

1. Chapter 1: the failure this book prevents, with one worked case
2. Chapter 2: the method, stated in one page
3. Chapters 3-6: one chapter per step, each ending in an exercise
4. Chapter 7: three complete examples
5. Chapter 8: what to do when the method does not fit
6. Appendix: worksheets, glossary, sources

Each chapter: claim, evidence, exercise, one paragraph summary. No chapter without an
exercise, because an exercise is what separates a book from a blog post.

## Deliverables checklist

- [ ] manuscript in Markdown, single source of truth
- [ ] PDF with working table of contents and page numbers
- [ ] EPUB only after the PDF sells, not before
- [ ] cover with title, subtitle, author, edition
- [ ] worksheets as separate files so they can be printed
- [ ] sources section with links, no unsourced numbers

## Sales page copy structure

1. Headline: the method plus the outcome
2. The two paragraphs that prove you have done the work
3. Table of contents, verbatim
4. Who should not buy this
5. Price, format, delivery
6. Refund sentence
7. FAQ
