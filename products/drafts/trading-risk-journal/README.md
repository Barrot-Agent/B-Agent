## What it is

A journal and a position-size calculator. The buyer records the plan before a session,
the execution during it, and the review after. The calculator answers one question:
given an account value and a risk percentage, what position size follows. It never
answers what to trade.

## Outline

1. What this measures and what it cannot
2. Before: thesis, invalidation, max loss, planned size
3. During: fill, slippage, deviations from plan
4. After: outcome, process score, one lesson
5. Four synthetic example weeks, clearly labelled synthetic
6. Worked risk arithmetic with three account sizes
