# Citation Integrity Playbook - DRAFT

Status: DRAFT. Not published. Awaiting approval.

## Problem

Research features ship citations that do not exist, or cite a paper that was
retracted after publication. The playbook is the procedure for catching both before
a user does, using checks a reader can re-run.

## Outline

1. What a citation claim is, and what it is not
2. Quote existence: substring, not paraphrase
3. Retraction checks: DOI, notice, and the trap of the wrong notice
4. Independence: why three sources that cite each other are one source
5. Verdict structure: letter, falsifier, upgrade condition, stated limit
6. Worked case: a document where 7 of 9 claims survived
7. Checklist and refusal rules
