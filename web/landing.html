Research Basis and Limits

Published work can inform research design, but academic findings do not validate Barrot forecasts or establish that a particular XRP feature predicts returns in a new period.

Settlement and payment rails

Settlement efficiency, liquidity usage, custody, and payment throughput are separate measurements. Greater XRPL or stablecoin activity does not by itself establish incremental XRP purchases, collateral demand, or token price impact.

Sentiment and market prediction

Published sentiment studies are asset-, sample-, and horizon-dependent. They are candidate explanatory evidence, not a substitute for synchronized point-in-time observations and leakage-safe out-of-sample tests against naive, momentum, mean-reversion, and volatility baselines.
