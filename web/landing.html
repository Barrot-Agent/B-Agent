Research Basis

This service isn't built on hunches. The two ideas behind it — that cross-border settlement efficiency matters, and that news/sentiment signals carry real predictive information — are both active areas of peer-reviewed academic research.

Settlement efficiency

Academic case studies comparing blockchain-based settlement models (including Ripple's) against traditional cross-border payment rails have found measurably lower time and capital costs, alongside greater transparency, versus legacy correspondent banking. Network-topology research has independently studied the structure of real Ripple transaction data. Separately, liquidity research places XRP in a distinct liquidity cluster alongside Bitcoin and Litecoin, identifying it as a leading contributor to short-run liquidity dynamics in that group.

Sentiment and news-driven signals

Published research on cryptocurrency sentiment has consistently found real predictive power for future returns — with one key condition: it only works when sentiment is measured with a domain-specific methodology, not generic scoring. Studies analyzing Twitter sentiment across major cryptocurrencies (XRP included) found genuine predictive power for several of them. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index has separately been shown to Granger-predict returns for major coins, with a stable long-term relationship between sentiment and price.
