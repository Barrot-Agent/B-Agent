# Ripple’s Momentum: Why XRP Is Gaining Traction Across Markets and Infrastructure

Moomoo’s recent analyst coverage has thrust Ripple back into the spotlight for retail traders, offering a fresh narrative that blends market visibility with tangible network upgrades. The platform’s report highlights the growing interest in XRP among both speculative participants and those looking for a bridge to institutional finance, creating a feedback loop that could amplify liquidity on the exchange.

On the technical front, the XRP Ledger has completed a significant upgrade that restores several functionalities previously disabled due to critical bugs. This rollout signals a renewed confidence among developers, who now have a more stable foundation for building payment applications, smart contract extensions, and tokenized assets. The restored features also reduce friction for existing use‑cases, such as real‑time settlement and multi‑currency routing, making the ledger more attractive for enterprises evaluating blockchain alternatives to traditional correspondent banking.

A parallel narrative is emerging from the institutional side. An analyst has projected that if large financial entities begin posting XRP as collateral, the resulting demand could push the network’s total market value toward the $100 trillion range. While the figure is speculative, it underscores the strategic value that banks and asset managers could derive from a highly liquid, low‑latency digital asset. The potential to use XRP as a collateral layer aligns with broader trends in decentralized finance, where on‑chain assets are increasingly integrated into margin and lending frameworks.

The cross‑border payment ecosystem provides a concrete illustration of that potential. Recent assessments of XRP’s adoption reveal a “ping‑pong” cycle where transaction volume, network reliability, and partnership depth reinforce each other. The current stage of this cycle can be described as a multi‑model refinement that has successfully passed two of four critical milestones: (1) proof of concept deployments with major remittance corridors, and (2) integration of on‑chain settlement with legacy banking APIs. The next phases will focus on scaling transaction throughput and achieving regulatory harmonization across jurisdictions, both of which are essential for moving from niche pilots to mainstream usage.

Institutional confidence is further cemented by SBI Holdings’ valuation of its Ripple stake at $41.2 billion. This assessment, issued by a leading Japanese financial conglomerate, reflects a deep belief in Ripple’s long‑term role in the global payments infrastructure. SBI’s stake valuation not only adds a credible price anchor for XRP but also signals that large, diversified financial groups are willing to allocate capital to blockchain‑based settlement solutions. The market has responded positively, with XRP price action reflecting the optimism generated by the valuation.

Taken together, these developments create a confluence of factors that could accelerate XRP’s trajectory. Enhanced visibility from retail platforms like Moomoo draws new participants, while the ledger’s technical robustness reassures developers and enterprise partners. Institutional interest, illustrated by both speculative collateral scenarios and concrete stake valuations, provides a macro‑level endorsement that could attract further capital inflows. Finally, the ongoing ping‑pong cycle demonstrates that the network is moving beyond experimental pilots toward a more mature, multi‑model payment architecture.

For fintech professionals and crypto investors, the key takeaway is that Ripple is no longer confined to a single narrative. It sits at the intersection of retail enthusiasm, developer activity, and institutional strategy. Monitoring the next two stages of the cross‑border adoption cycle—scaling throughput and achieving regulatory alignment—will be crucial for gauging whether the current momentum translates into sustained market share. As the ecosystem matures, XRP’s role as a bridge between traditional finance and decentralized networks is likely to become increasingly prominent.
