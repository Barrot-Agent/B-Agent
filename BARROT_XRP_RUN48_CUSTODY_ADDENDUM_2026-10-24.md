# Run 48 Addendum — Evernorth Custody, Collateral and Yield Strategy

**Research cutoff:** 2026-10-24.  
**Status:** Primary filing reviewed; actual deployed amount and transactions remain unverified.

## Primary-source facts

Evernorth's SEC prospectus dated October 19, 2026 states:
- All XRP is held through BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A. (formerly BitGo Trust Company) as primary custodian.
- As of the prospectus date, 100% of XRP is maintained in offline/cold storage.
- The company intends to lend XRP to qualified institutional counterparties for interest, use covered-call strategies and basis trades, supply XRP to DeFi lending protocols, and contribute liquidity to automated market-maker pools.
- It intends to use RLUSD in XRP-based DeFi, including RLUSD/XRP AMM pools.
- The prospectus says yield strategies commenced following the October 9 closing, while also saying 100% of XRP was in cold storage as of the prospectus date and that material ecosystem deployment was not anticipated before Q4 2026.
- It may consider term loans or other financing collateralized by unencumbered XRP, subject to market conditions and legal constraints, and may monetize or sell holdings to fund operations or strategic opportunities.

## Interpretation boundaries

The prospectus documents custody arrangements and stated strategy, not the amount of XRP actually lent, pledged, hedged, deposited in DeFi or sold after closing. The phrase that yield strategies commenced after closing and the statement that 100% remained in cold storage as of the prospectus date create a timing/definition question that requires later disclosures or transaction-level evidence. Do not infer that the full 473M XRP is actively deployed or, conversely, permanently unavailable to the market.

Potential liquidity mechanisms:
1. **Cold storage / treasury retention:** could reduce immediately tradable float if tokens remain unencumbered and are not sold or lent.
2. **Institutional lending:** may place XRP with counterparties, potentially changing lendable supply, collateral reuse and hedge activity; actual effects depend on agreements and counterparties.
3. **Covered calls and basis trades:** can create derivatives exposure and hedge flows; they do not automatically imply spot buying or selling of a fixed size.
4. **DeFi lending and AMM pools:** can increase on-ledger liquidity and token availability while introducing smart-contract, liquidation and pool-imbalance risks.
5. **XRP-backed financing:** could allow capital raising without selling XRP, but introduces collateral, haircut, margin-call and liquidation pathways.

## Required evidence next

- Subsequent Evernorth filings and investor disclosures showing XRP purchases, holdings, deployed percentages, lending, collateral, sales or yield performance.
- Custody/transaction disclosures sufficient to distinguish cold-storage balances from operational or deployed balances.
- Counterparty and collateral terms where publicly disclosed, including haircuts, margining, rehypothecation rights and liquidation conditions.
- RLUSD/XRP pool depth, deposits/withdrawals, trading volume and concentration around any announced deployment.
- Compare actual flows against the hypothesis that treasury holdings mechanically remove supply from circulation.

## Forecast decision

No XRP price forecast issued from this filing alone. These are strategy and custody disclosures, not a quantified market-flow observation. Any causal claim about price impact requires verified deployment amounts and synchronized spot, derivatives and market-depth data.
