## Budget Optimisation Tips

1. **Buy magnets in bulk:** 100-pack N52 magnets saves ~30% vs. individual
2. **Print slowly:** Higher quality = fewer failed prints = less wasted filament
3. **Reconditioned hub motors:** E-bike repair shops often have used motors
4. **DIY coils:** Winding your own electromagnets saves $80 vs. buying pre-made
5. **Community sourcing:** Partner with a local makerspace for printer access
