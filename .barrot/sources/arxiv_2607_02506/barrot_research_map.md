### BARROT RESEARCH HYPOTHESES
These are NOT claims made by the paper.

1. A Barrot execution/knowledge graph might exhibit analogous
   localized "hotspots" where a small perturbation produces
   disproportionate downstream propagation.

2. Dependency propagation, knowledge propagation, failure propagation,
   and execution propagation may be measurable as graph-front processes.

3. A future Barrot propagation model could distinguish:
   - propagation velocity
   - propagation-front broadening
   - local amplification
   - stochastic uncertainty

4. The paper's Markov-process reduction may provide a useful
   mathematical template for reduced stochastic models of Barrot
   execution or knowledge propagation.

5. The integrability-breaking parameter should NOT be mapped directly
   to an existing Barrot variable without independent validation.
